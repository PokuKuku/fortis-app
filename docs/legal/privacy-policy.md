# Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 3, 2026

Fortis AI Workout Tracker Log ("Fortis", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by Kwaku Bimpong. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Fortis AI Workout Tracker Log mobile app (the "App").

Contact: fortisfitness@gmail.com

## Information we collect

### Account information
- Email address and name.
- Authentication details from Apple or Google sign-in (we do not receive your password).

### Profile and onboarding information
- Gender, birth date/age, height, weight, preferred units.
- Lifting experience, workouts per week, fitness goals, challenges, and workout consistency.
- How you heard about the app and whether you have tried other apps.

### Workout and fitness data
- Workout programs and templates.
- Workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest timer data, notes, and history.
- Bodyweight history and other fitness metrics you log.

### Usage and device information
- App interactions, features used, and performance analytics.
- Device identifiers and basic device information (for analytics and security).

### AI feature inputs
- Messages or prompts you send to the AI, and relevant workout context used to generate responses.

### Purchases and subscriptions
- Subscription status and purchase metadata provided by Apple (we do not collect or store full payment details).

### Notifications
- Push notification token if you enable notifications.

### Support communications
- Messages you send to us (e.g., support emails).

## How we use information
We use information to:
- Provide and operate the App and its features.
- Personalize your experience and generate workout insights.
- Provide AI-powered assistance and recommendations.
- Process subscriptions and confirm entitlements.
- Send notifications you opt in to receive.
- Monitor usage, improve performance, and fix issues.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## How we share information
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed:
- **Service providers** that help us operate the App, such as:
  - Supabase (database, authentication, storage)
  - Mixpanel (analytics)
  - OpenAI (AI processing)
  - Apple and Google (sign-in and purchases)
- **Legal and safety**: If required by law, to protect rights, or prevent fraud or abuse.
- **With your consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing.

## Data retention
We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. You can delete your account in the App (Settings -> Danger Zone). Deleting your account removes your data from our systems, except where we must keep records for legal or security reasons or in backups for a limited time.

## Security
We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure. Please keep your login credentials secure.

## Your choices
- Update or correct your profile in the App.
- Opt out of push notifications in device settings.
- Delete your account in the App (Settings -> Danger Zone).

## Children's privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## International transfers
Your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date.
